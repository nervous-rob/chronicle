# Chronicle implementation status — plans-v1 (multi-day plans as a policy reply)

## Increment: plans (goal + ordered steps + assumptions, executed by the engine, broken by the world)
- **`Agency.plans`** (`agency.py`): one in-flight plan per person, stored as plain JSON — `goal` (`kind` from the closed `plans.goal_kinds` vocabulary, free `why` ≤ `plans.max_chars`), ordered `steps` (each an ordinary menu action, optional `expects` as dotted packet path + value), `assumptions` (dotted packet paths that must keep holding), `made_day`, `horizon_day`, `cursor`, `author`, and the ledger mark at adoption. Steps run through the ordinary `_resolve`, so legality is exactly what a direct action would face (a plan step that hauls from a granary one does not stand in is refused the same way). The first step runs on adoption day; adoption itself costs no time. A `plan` step inside a plan is rejected (never nested).
- **`plan` meta-verb** (`config.VERBS`, `agency.py` `_offer_plan` / `_do_plan`): untimed, `requires = None`; rides the registry like every other verb (menu order is still a registry walk, `_menu`/`_resolve` have no per-verb branch). The offer carries the caller's caps: `max_steps`, `horizon_days`, `goal_kinds`, `max_assumptions`.
- **`[plans]` config** (`run.default.toml`, validated in `config.py`): `base_steps` 2 / `base_horizon_days` 3 for someone without a plan-enabling capability; `steps_per_level` 4 / `horizon_days_per_level` 11 added per planning level; `max_assumptions` 6; `max_chars` 200; `goal_kinds` closed vocabulary (theme data — the suite swaps it with zero Python); `surprise_events` list validated against `EVENT_TYPES`. Rejections: unknown surprise event, zero base steps, empty goal vocabulary, unknown key. Over-long plans are truncated to the cap and the truncation is logged, not refused.
- **`capabilities.planning`** (`run.default.toml`): `learn_days` 60, `min_stage` youth, `teachers` any_holder, no medium, `enables = ["plan"]` (`plan` joins `CAPABILITY_PRIMITIVES`). Level = 0 without any capability enabling `plan`, 1 with one: 2 steps / 3 days vs 6 steps / 14 days. The engine never names the capability (asserted by the suite); it never gates legality — only step cap and horizon.
- **Breaks** (checked each morning before a step, in `agency.py`): `finished`, `expired` (past `horizon_day` with steps left), `step_refused` (yesterday's step was refused by the resolver; the refusal is remembered on the plan), `expectation` (a step's `expects` does not hold next morning), `assumption` (an adoption-time-true assumption stops holding), `surprise` (a newly remembered event of a `plans.surprise_events` type since adoption, e.g. `found_asleep`), `critical` (a critical body band). On a break the plan is dropped and the policy is woken with `packet.plan.break` naming the kind; the vocabulary is asserted to be exactly these seven. A policy may answer a break with a new plan. This is the ask-trigger policy for the contact/commitment increment.
- **Decision log**: every record gains a `plan` field (`made_day`, `step`, `of`, `break`, `truncated`); plan-executed days log `source: "plan:<author>"` and make no policy call. Policy call pattern for a 3-step plan adopted day 0: consulted days 0 and 3 (break `finished`), silent on days 1–2.
- **Checkpoint `VERSION = 3`** (`checkpoint.py`): `Agency.plans` serialized and validated (malformed plan records are refused). A reloaded engine keeps the in-flight plan with no policy attached; resuming mid-plan and replaying the log reproduces the uninterrupted world and log bit-identically over 30 days. Both sleep-v1a loose ends are closed: `checkpoints/example-day-5.zip` regenerated at v3, and `tests/test_checkpoints.py` now stamps the real `checkpoint.VERSION` into its report (`schema_version: 3`).

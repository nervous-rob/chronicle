# Chronicle implementation status — contact-v1 (hearsay: memory moves between people only by telling)

## Increment: contact phase + the `tell` primitive (what gets said, to whom, at what cost, and what it is worth)
- **`people.tell(day, teller, listener, eid, *, overheard=False)`** (`people.py`): the ONLY way a memory moves from one person to another; words alone move nothing. It ledgers one private `tell` event (`actors = [teller, listener]`, payload `event`, `fidelity`, `hops`, `overheard`) and writes a hearsay record in the listener at `fidelity × gossip.tell_fidelity_mult` (× `gossip.crowd_fidelity` when overheard), `source = "hearsay"`, `hops = teller's hops + 1`, `from = teller`. `remember()` keeps its single rule — a record is replaced only by a strictly higher fidelity — so a better source upgrades (and resets hops to its own) and a worse source never downgrades a witness; a second telling at the same or lower fidelity changes nothing. Telling oneself or telling what one does not remember raises. The teller keeps the original.
- **Memory records** are now `{day, fidelity, source, hops, from}` everywhere (witnessed: `hops 0, from None`). `people.can_remember(p, day)` is the one rule (alive, named, at least child) used by `remember` and by the contact phase alike.
- **`people.salience(e)`**: `[gossip.salience]` lookup with the same shape as `[events.witness]` — `"type:flag"` (truthy payload flag), then `type`, then `default` (required). **`people.tellable(teller, listener, gen)`**: picks ONE remembered event the listener lacks (or holds below what a telling would give), weighted by `salience × hop_decay^hops`; `None` when nothing is worth telling (zero salience is never chosen; `hop_decay 0` means second-hand memories are never passed on).
- **`engine._contact_phase(d)`** (`engine.py`), after agency decisions and before `_sleep_resolve`. Co-presence = same `(cell, place)`; open ground is one commons per cell (`place = -1`) at `contact.commons_mult`. People who are busy, on a journey, or cannot remember are not present. For each slot and each group of ≥ 2: every member with `sent < send_cap` and at least `hours_per_exchange` left draws from `rng.one('contact', day, (pid << 16) | slot)` — rolls are keyed by day/person/slot, never by draw order, so an irrelevant change elsewhere in the world leaves every exchange identical (tested). Initiation chance `p_base` (× `commons_mult` on open ground); the listener is a weighted choice among co-present people under `receive_cap` with hours left (`same_household_mult` for a household member, 1.0 otherwise); `tellable` picks the event; `tell` moves it; both parties are charged `hours_per_exchange` through `farming._spend(..., 'talk', label=False)` so the day's rest/work bookkeeping is untouched; every other member under `receive_cap` overhears with `gossip.overhear_p` for free. One initiation per person per slot, slots are the outer loop. Nothing crosses a wall: a person next door is never a party.
- **`[contact]` config** (`run.default.toml`, validated in `config.py`): `slots` 2, `hours_per_exchange` 0.25 (≤ `agriculture.activity_hours_per_day`), `send_cap` 2, `receive_cap` 3, `p_base` 0.3, `commons_mult` 0.3, `same_household_mult` 1.5. **`[gossip]`** finally has a reader: `crowd_fidelity` 0.8, `tell_fidelity_mult` 0.9, `hop_decay` 0.8, `overhear_p` 0.2, `[gossip.salience]` table (keys validated against `EVENT_TYPES`, non-negative, `default` required); `gossip.rate_days` is a dead legacy cadence and is listed in `tests/test_config_coverage.py` `PENDING_KEYS` with its reason (candidate for removal) rather than given a fake reader. **`[events.witness]`** gains scope `"none"` (`tell = "none"`): nobody auto-remembers the event, not even the actors — the primitive writes the hearsay record itself. Rejections tested: `p_base` > 1, unknown `[contact]` key, negative `hop_decay`, unknown witness scope, exchange longer than the day, negative salience. Asserted: no engine code reads or sets a person tier — contact rates come from role, ties and place only.
